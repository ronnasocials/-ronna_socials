Most clinics have the expertise. Most just aren't posting it.

I work with dental practices to plan, create, and manage the content they already have the material for — treatments, transformations, and patient trust — and turn it into a steady posting rhythm designed to drive bookings, not just likes.

Services

What's included

01

Content strategy & calendar

Monthly content pillars and a posting calendar built around your services — Invisalign, veneers, implants, general care — mapped to what actually drives consults.

02

Content creation

Turning existing clips, before/afters, and clinic moments into short-form video and carousel posts, edited for Instagram and Facebook.

03

Posting & page management

Scheduling, captions, hashtags, and day-to-day upkeep so the page stays active without taking your time.

04

Engagement & DMs

Responding to comments and inquiries in a way that reflects the practice's tone and nudges toward booking.

Process

How a month works

1

Audit & angle

Review your current page, reviews, and services to find the content angle that fits your practice.

2

Calendar & approval

You get a month of planned content — pillars, formats, and captions — before anything goes live.

3

Create & schedule

Posts are edited and scheduled around your clinic's existing footage and photos.

4

Report & refine

Monthly check-in on what worked, adjusting the next month's plan around it.
